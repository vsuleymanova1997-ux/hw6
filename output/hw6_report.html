5. Recommendation to the store manager

  • Cost: the defended agent averaged $0.000228 per document on the exam set, so 1,000 documents a day for 30 days (30,000 documents) is about $6.83 per month (the undefended agent measured $23.24 per month on the practice set).
  • Safety on the practice set: 0 of 12 documents fooled the fully defended agent; trap tool calls on exam and practice runs: 0.
  • Red team: 2 of 2 new attacks fooled the undefended agent; 0 of 2 fooled the defended agent.

The judgment below is written by hand from the numbers above; the figures in the list are computed from the result files.

Recommendation: yes, turn it on for real vendor mail, as a drafting assistant with a buyer approving every recommendation, and with all three defense layers on.

Why yes: the cost is small (see the monthly figure above, a few dollars a month at 1,000 documents a day), the fully defended agent was not fooled by any of the 12 practice documents, no trap tool was ever called, and the clean quotes still came back right.

Why only with a buyer in the loop:

  • No single layer was enough. The code layer alone missed both of my new attacks, because it does not know those hiding tricks. The design layer alone was fooled whenever the planted figure looked like a normal revision, because the reader model copies it. The prompt layer stopped everything I tried, but it is a request to a model, not a guarantee. Keep all three on.
  • Nothing can catch a lie that sits in plain sight. A vendor who simply quotes a bad price, or a visible fake "correction", looks identical to a real one. The buyer is the check on that.
  • The exam results have no answer key, so they were not scored. A buyer should spot-check them, especially any recommend.
  • I tested 12 practice documents and 2 attacks of my own. A determined attacker has more tricks than that, so keep watching output/trap_calls.json: any entry means a document took over the agent.

Keep send_email and approve_payment as logging-only until the buyer step exists, and never connect the agent to real payment or email. Turn it on for incoming quotes first, review the first month of recommendations by hand, then decide whether to widen it.
